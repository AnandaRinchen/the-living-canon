# The Living Canon

**A Seven-Year Journey Through the Words of the Buddha**

For the first time in history, half of the Kangyur — the 108 volumes of the Buddha's direct teachings — exists in English. *The Living Canon* is an unprecedented, global reading group journeying through every completed page over seven transformative years.

## About

- 📖 12–13 pages of contemplative reading per day
- 🎥 Weekly 90-minute live Zoom sessions every Sunday
- 📝 Written study guides and daily Tibetan insights
- 🌍 A global community of practitioners
