The Invitation

The beating heart of the
Tibetan Buddhist tradition —
now within reach.

For centuries, the Kangyur has lived behind the twin barriers of classical Tibetan and monastic walls — studied meticulously by scholars, yet remaining beyond the reach of the everyday practitioner. Its 108 volumes contain the direct teachings of the Buddha: the foundational wisdom that gave rise to every lineage, every practice, every aspiration toward awakening.

Today, thanks to historic and ongoing translation efforts, a profound milestone has been reached. We now have the means — and the moment — to bring these words to the world.

50%
Translated

Exactly half of the Kangyur has now been rigorously translated into English. This is a historic moment — tens of thousands of pages, reviewed by teams of scholars and translators working across decades. To honor this milestone, The Living Canon will read every single completed page over the next seven years.

Source: 84000 — Translating the Words of the Buddha · ~35,000 pages now in English

The Seven-Year Map

From the first turning of the
wheel to the esoteric tantras.

Over seven years, we journey through the entire breadth of the Buddha's teachings as preserved in the Kangyur — foundational wisdom, visionary cosmology, profound ethics, and esoteric practice.

Year One — The Foundation
The Vinaya & the First Turning of the Wheel
Vinayavastu · Dharmacakra Pravartana Sūtra

We begin where the Buddha began — with the foundational discourses and the monastic code that shaped the earliest sangha. The Four Noble Truths, the Eightfold Path, and the ethical bedrock of the entire tradition.

EthicsFour Noble Truths12 pp/day
Year Two — Dependent Origination
The Sūtra Collections & Causality
Śālistamba Sūtra · Nidāna texts

Diving into the sūtra collections that map the web of dependent origination — karma, causality, and the twelve links. Here we learn to see how our actions shape reality and how to interrupt the cycle of suffering.

CausalityTwelve Links12 pp/day
Year Three — The Perfection of Wisdom
The Heart Sūtra & the Prajñāpāramitā
Prajñāpāramitāhṛdaya · Aṣṭasāhasrikā

The radical heart of the Mahāyāna. We confront emptiness directly — deconstructing rigid views and learning to hold the paradox of form and formlessness as a lived, daily practice of wisdom and freedom.

EmptinessŚūnyatā13 pp/day
Year Four — Cutting Through Illusion
The Diamond Sūtra & the Bodhisattva Ideal
Vajracchedikā Prajñāpāramitā

Building on the wisdom of Year Three, we turn the diamond cutter on the self itself. Merit, generosity, and the Bodhisattva's vow to liberate all beings — living actively in the world without being bound by it.

Bodhisattva PathNon-attachment13 pp/day
Year Five — The Jewel Heap
The Mahāratnakūṭa Collection
Mahāratnakūṭa Sūtra

One of the most diverse and ethically rich collections in the Kangyur. Forty-nine independent sūtras exploring purity, devotion, skillful means, and the vast range of the Buddha's teachings to different audiences and capacities.

EthicsSkillful Means12 pp/day
Year Six — The Flower Ornament
The Avataṃsaka & Visionary Cosmology
Avataṃsaka Sūtra · Gaṇḍavyūha

The most visionary and vast text in all of Buddhism. An infinite net of interpenetrating realities, the pilgrim Sudhana's journey through fifty-three spiritual teachers, and a cosmology that shatters every boundary of scale and imagination.

CosmologyInterpenetration13 pp/day
Year Seven — The Esoteric Foundation
The Tantra Collections
Kriyā · Caryā · Yoga · Anuttarayoga Tantra

The culmination. We enter the esoteric dimension of the Kangyur — ritual, visualization, mantra, and the transformation of ordinary perception into awakened experience. A fitting crown to seven years of deepening practice.

TantraTransformation12 pp/day
